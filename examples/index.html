Sigil in the browser

Every glyph on this page is drawn from model.json at runtime.

1. <sigil-glyph>

The whole library as one element. It inherits the surrounding text colour.

2. Live

Type any non-negative integer. Past 9999 the stem grows a row rather than splitting into two glyphs.

3. Canvas — many at once

120 glyphs in a single canvas. This is why the Canvas renderer exists: one path per glyph rather than a DOM node per line.
